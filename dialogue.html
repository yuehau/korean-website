Act 1 — Group Call (three friends deciding what to eat)

Huiyee

오늘 뭐 먹을까요?

What should we eat today?

Yuehao

저는 비빔밥 먹고 싶어요.

I want to eat bibimbap.

Yunyi

김밥도 맛있어요.

Gimbap is delicious too.

Huiyee

김치는 어때요?

How about kimchi?

Yuehao

김치는 꼭 있어야 해요!

We definitely need kimchi!

Yunyi

IOI 시티몰 어때요?

How about IOI City Mall?

Yuehao

좋아요! 거기 교촌치킨도 있어요.

Sounds good! They have KyoChon Chicken there too.

Huiyee

저는 교촌치킨 좋아해요. 그럼 IOI 시티몰 교촌치킨으로 가요!

I like KyoChon Chicken. Then let's go to KyoChon at IOI City Mall!

Everyone

좋아요! 이따 만나요!

Great! See you later!

Act 2 — At KyoChon Chicken Restaurant, IOI City Mall

(The three arrive at the KyoChon restaurant and sit down. Server Yihong approaches.)

Server Yihong

어서 오세요. 여기 앉으세요. 메뉴 여기 있어요.

Welcome. Please sit here. Here's the menu. (hands over the menu)

Huiyee

네, 감사합니다.

Yes, thank you.

(After looking at the menu for a while, Yuehao raises a hand to order.)

Yuehao

여기요! 주문할게요.

Excuse me! We'd like to order.

Server Yihong

네, 말씀하세요.

Yes, go ahead.

Yunyi

치킨 아홉 개하고 콜라 세 잔 주세요.

Please give us nine chickens and three cups of cola.

Huiyee

그리고 김밥 한 그릇하고 비빔밥 두 그릇 주세요.

And please give us one bowl of gimbap and two bowls of bibimbap.

Yuehao

아, 김치 세 그릇도 주세요!

Oh, please also give us three bowls of kimchi!

Server Yihong

아홉 개, 콜라 세 잔, 김밥 한 그릇, 비빔밥 두 그릇, 김치 세 그릇 맞아요?

Nine chickens, three cups of cola, one bowl of gimbap, two bowls of bibimbap, three bowls of kimchi — is that correct?

Yuehao

네, 맞아요. 그렇게 주세요.

Yes, that's correct. Please prepare it that way.

Server Yihong

네, 잠깐만 기다리세요.

Okay, please wait a moment.

Everyone

감사합니다!

Thank you!
